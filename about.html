Where I am from

Born in Ranchi, Jharkhand. I live in Khalari, near Dhanbad, Jharkhand, India. A small town, but that has never limited what I can build. Everything I make is online and available to anyone, anywhere.

What I do

Full-stack web development and AI automation. Along with my studies I spend time on coding, automation and building products. I learn by doing, not just by reading.

What I like

Clean design, fast software, and systems that run without me watching them. I like it when a project just works.

What I want

To build products that people use every day, to keep learning, and to make technology feel simple.

Build first

I ship early. A working version teaches more than a perfect plan. I would rather have something online that I can improve than an idea that stays in my head.

Automate repeat work

If a task repeats, I turn it into a system. That is the whole point of my Instagram agent and my scripts — they do the boring part so I can focus on the next thing.

Keep it simple

Simple code is easier to fix and easier to grow. I avoid adding things I do not need, and I remove anything that does not help.

Test everything

I check my work myself before I show it to anyone. If it breaks, I fix it and I write down what went wrong so I do not repeat it.

Learn in public

I share my work on GitHub and on my sites. Feedback from real users is the fastest way to learn.

Stay curious

I try new tools often. Most of them I drop, but the good ones change how I work.
